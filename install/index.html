Ripe-O-Matic · install

Put the firmware on an ESP32 straight from this page. Nothing to install on your computer. Works in Chrome or Edge on a desktop or laptop (the browser needs USB access, which phones and Firefox/Safari don't give).

1 · Plug the ESP32 into this computer

Any ESP32 DevKit (WROOM, 30- or 38-pin). Use a USB cable that carries data: many charging cables don't, and the board then never shows up.

2 · Install

Click, pick the board's port in the popup (usually the only one listed, "CP2102" or "CH340"), and wait about a minute. When it asks, enter your Wi-Fi; it then links straight to your device.

3 · Open it

After the Wi-Fi step the dialog shows a Visit device button. Bookmark it, or open http://ripeomatic.local on your phone and add it to the home screen. The device page then walks you through the rest one step at a time: phone alerts, a motor direction check, the two calibrations, the first avocado.

If it doesn't go

  • No port in the list: try another cable or USB port. On Windows the board may need the CP210x or CH340 driver, depending on the little chip next to the USB connector.
  • "Failed to initialize" / stuck at connecting: hold the board's BOOT button, click Install, release BOOT once it starts writing.
  • Another program has the port: close the Arduino IDE serial monitor or any other terminal.
  • Skipped the Wi-Fi step, or it failed: the device starts its own network, RipeOMatic-xxxxxx. Join it from your phone and a setup page opens.
  • Something else: the Logs & console option in the dialog shows what the board prints. Paste that into an issue.
